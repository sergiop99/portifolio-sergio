01 / AI & SAAS

Interviewiz ↗

An interview preparation idea became a live AI subscription product.

My role

I led delivery end to end. We defined the product, subscription model and architecture, then built the platform.

The technical decision

Model calls took about 20 seconds and blocked the interface. I moved them to asynchronous processing, designed subscription logic and added API usage limits.

10,000+monthly sessions within weeks of launch

Billing operated without reported incidents during that early launch period.

02 / API PLATFORM & DELIVERY

NextBlock ↗

A fixed launch date needed a smaller, agreed first release.

The scope decision

In the first week, we moved noncritical features to version two. The founder approved what would stay out before development began.

What we delivered

A React dashboard, self-service API keys, usage metering, four subscription tiers with Stripe, rate limiting and global routing.

Day 28product launch

Approximately US$250,000 processed by the platform in its first 60 days.
